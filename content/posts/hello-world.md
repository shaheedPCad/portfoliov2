This is the first post on the site. It's intentionally simple: a Markdown file with frontmatter, rendered through `next-mdx-remote`.

## Why minimal

No hero sections, no cards, no animations — just text, dates, and links. The goal is to keep the focus on content rather than chrome.

## What's next

More posts will go here as `.md` files in `content/posts`, each one picked up automatically by the blog index.
